Step-by-Step Election Day Voting Procedure

8:30 AM - 2:30 PM

Polling units open nationwide at 8:30 AM. Accreditation and voting commence simultaneously and conclude at 2:30 PM. However, every voter on the queue on or before 2:30 PM is entitled to vote.

⚠️Strict Policy: NO PVC, NO VOTING! All voters must be physically present and verified through the BVAS device.
1Arrival

Join the Orderly Queue

Arrive at your registered Polling Unit and join the orderly queue. Ensure you have your physical Permanent Voter Card (PVC) with you.

2Verification

Present Your PVC

Present your Permanent Voter Card (PVC) to the Assistant Presiding Officer (APO II) who scans the card details on the BVAS reader.

3Biometrics

BVAS Biometric Accreditation

The BVAS captures and verifies your fingerprint biometrics. If fingerprint capture fails, facial recognition authentication is instantly deployed.

4Issuance

Register Check & Ballot Paper

The Assistant Presiding Officer (APO I) verifies your name on the register, marks the register, signs and stamps the official ballot paper, and hands it to you.

5Secrecy

Thumbprint in Secret

Proceed to the private voting cubicle. Thumbprint cleanly inside the box corresponding to your chosen political party symbol using indelible ink.

6 & 7Casting & Observation

Cast Ballot & Observe Peacefully

Fold your ballot vertically inward and drop it into the designated ballot box. You may leave or remain at least 300 meters away to observe open counting and results announcement.
