## 2. Plain Statement on Generation & Template Logic

> **CRITICAL DISCLOSURE**:  
> In the current prototype repository, **generation is NOT performed by an unconstrained neural LLM hallucinating text**.  
> Instead, all answers are generated by **deterministic slot-filling templates** in `src/generation/prompt_builder.py` that extract the verbatim legal text from gazetted chunks, insert the section numbers, enforce unreviewed glossary terms, and append statutory citations and gazette dates.  
> This guarantees zero hallucinations and 100% statutory traceability, but means the natural language phrasing is predetermined rather than generated on-the-fly by an LLM.

## 3. Pre-Registered Decision Rule for Retrieval Model Selection

Before executing the 5-configuration Colab benchmark comparison (Configs A, B, C, D, E) across the complete frozen 33-query evaluation benchmark (20 retrieval queries, 8 held-out queries, 5 natural queries), the architectural selection rule is pre-registered:

1. **Reranker Selection Criterion**: The cross-encoder reranker (`BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3`, Config D or E) will be activated in production over dense-only retrieval (`BAAI/bge-m3`, Config B) **ONLY IF it strictly beats Config B on overall Top-1 accuracy** across the full 33-query benchmark.
2. **Tie-Breaking & Latency Governance**: If Top-1 accuracy is identical between dense-only (Config B) and reranked pipelines (Config D or E), the lower-latency dense configuration (Config B or hybrid Config C) shall be selected, avoiding the ~15ms per candidate cross-encoder compute tax.
3. **Hybrid Fusion Criterion**: Config C (BM25 + BGE-M3 RRF) will be selected over Config B (Dense only) if its Hit@3 or MRR is strictly higher without degrading Top-1 accuracy.
